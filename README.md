# Cyber-portfolio
A portfolio detailing previous CTF and cyber experiences 
Background - After separating from the military I was at a crossroads, I wanted to move away from being a Police Officer and began transitioning towards a career in IT. As I began to look into career paths cyber defense stood out to me, The material I read was extremely interesting to me and I felt that it would be a good way to combine my security background with a new career in IT. I enrolled in a local college's cybersecurity degree path, which served as a great foundation of networking and cyber concepts before I decided to pursue higher education at SANS. 

NCL FALL 2026 - The start of this CTF was strange, I had previously competed in beginner bracket of the Spring 2025 games and although I definitely knew more, the increased difficulty of the experienced bracket had me feeling oddly similar to how I felt during the first CTF, unsure. As I dove into the games I began to relax a bit, Thankfully I built up a stronger foundation of skills throughout the year and a half gap between games. My networking and log analysis background gave me a bit of a confidence boost as well. While I may not have been able to achieve a 100% completion rate, I was satisfied with how much I improved, having struggled to get a 60% last game to pretty comfortably reaching my 70% goal this time.

Without going into the specifics of the challenge due to NCL rules, one highlight of the game for me was my web app performance. Historically Web Application Exploitation has been one of the hardest categories for me due to it being the category I have the least experience in. During the initial prep leading up to the start of the individual and team games I consumed a vast amount information from official sources, like the NCL youtube page as well as videos provided by SANS and other third party resources to try to address this weakness. While I may not have been able to solve every web challenge, I noticed an improvement from my previous attempt. Additionally during the team games we found out that web app was our teams weakest link and I was able to utilize the knowledge I gained to crack the first challenge, which unfortunately ended up being the only one we finished but I was still happy to see my research pay off.
